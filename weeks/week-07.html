Weekly Recap

Week 7 Recap (2026-11-09 — 2026-11-15)

Tuesday, November 17, 2026

Matchup Recaps

by Dale Prychitko

Beef edged Kegstands 232.8-220.9, and it came down to who got more out of the skaters. Kegstands had the goaltending week: Juuse Saros led the roster with 27.0 fantasy points, the goalies piled up 188 saves and two shutouts, and four wins came with them. It still wasn't enough, because Beef got 36.0 points from Kyle Connor on the left side and 29.3 from Nikita Kucherov. Victor Olofsson added 25.0 from the wing. Beef had only 90 saves and two wins from the crease, so the forwards did nearly all the work. Elite goaltending couldn't close an 11.9-point gap.

I Walk for Sea Shanties took down Walter White Walker 172.1-159.9, the lowest-scoring game on the card. John Carlson's 26.8 fantasy points from the back end led I Walk for Sea Shanties, with Ivar Stenberg of SJ adding 18.8 and Sam Bennett of FLA 16.5. The goalie slots barely registered: 12 saves and one goal against all week, which tells you where the lineup got its points. Walter White Walker had four wins and a shutout from its goalies, but its skaters managed just 68 shots and 33 hits. Roman Josi's 16.8 points led the roster, and nobody else got to the top end.

dMC beat armdawg 213.0-195.1, and the lead changed hands early before settling. armdawg was up through Monday and again on Wednesday, when it pushed ahead 74.6-55.2. dMC answered Thursday and kept the lead the rest of the way. Sam Reinhart of FLA led the roster with 27.8 fantasy points, Lucas Raymond of DET had 22.8, and Matvei Michkov of PHI had 19.0. The shot volume mattered too, with 119 on goal and 39 blocks. armdawg got 28.5 from Connor McDavid and 21.6 from Jacob Markstrom, but its skaters recorded only 24 hits.

Hammer held off Corey's Crazy Team 223.6-204.3 after a week of swings. Corey's Crazy Team led after Monday, Hammer took over Tuesday and Wednesday, and Corey's Crazy Team went back in front Thursday and Friday. Hammer retook the lead Saturday at 194.1-176.5 and finished it Sunday. Brandon Hagel of TB led the roster with 32.8 fantasy points, and Joel Hofer of STL added 24.6 from the net. Macklin Celebrini of SJ had 30.8 for Corey's Crazy Team, and Jake Guentzel of TB had 24.5. Corey's Crazy Team got no wins from its goalies, and that is hard to overcome.

Slew Foot Slim beat HabsNation 247.2-207.1. Slew Foot Slim was in front Monday and Tuesday, then trailed 87.1-86.6 on Wednesday. Thursday it moved back ahead 155.5-124.6 and never gave the lead up again. Simon Holmstrom of NYI led the roster with 28.8 fantasy points, Cole Hutson of WSH added 27.0, and Bo Horvat of NYI had 22.8. Slew Foot Slim scored 18 goals, the most of the week, and its 40 points tied MazerReed for the top mark. HabsNation had 66 hits and 36 blocks, with Artturi Lehkonen of COL leading the roster at 22.8, but it came up short on goals.

MazerReed took the wildest game of the week, beating Sheeting Carlsson 248.9-208.5 after four lead changes. MazerReed led Monday, Sheeting Carlsson went ahead Tuesday, and MazerReed took it back Wednesday at 78.1-68.8. Sheeting Carlsson was in front again Thursday and Friday, at 132.0-126.0 and then 150.4-141.2. Saturday finally went MazerReed's way, 210.9-194.0, and the lead grew from there. Leon Draisaitl of EDM led the roster with 38.0 fantasy points, the top individual mark in the division this week. Will Smith of SJ had 21.5, and Nikolaj Ehlers of CAR had 19.0. Matt Boldy of MIN led Sheeting Carlsson with 23.5.

zzz beat Charles 236.1-194.2, the biggest margin of the week at 41.9 points. Mark Scheifele of WPG led the roster with 32.5 fantasy points. Evan Bouchard of EDM had 23.8 and Mika Zibanejad of NYR had 23.3. Charles had four goalie wins, and Jesper Wallstedt of MIN led the roster with 26.7 fantasy points. The skaters gave little: six goals all week, and only 81 shots. zzz scored 16 goals, and that gap decided the matchup.

Players of the Week

by Dale Prychitko

MazerReed had the best skater week in the division, and Leon Draisaitl made it look routine. The EDM centre piled up 4 goals, 3 assists and 15 shots on goal for 38.0 fantasy points, with a shorthanded point and 3 blocks tucked in as a bonus. Seven points on the week is the headline, but the shot volume is what kept the meter running every night. Draisaitl is eligible at C, LW and RW, so MazerReed could slot him wherever the lineup needed him.

Two wingers pushed him to the wire. Kyle Connor of WPG gave Beef 36.0 fantasy points on the same 4 goals, 3 assists and 15 shots, plus 2 hits and 2 blocks. Brandon Hagel of TB turned 4 goals, 2 assists, 15 shots and 3 hits into 32.8 for Hammer. Mark Scheifele, Connor's WPG linemate, put up 8 points on 3 goals and 5 assists for zzz, good for 32.5.

The crease belonged to Kegstands, who had two of the week's top four goalies. Juuse Saros of NSH took the crown with 27.0 fantasy points: 90 saves on 96 shots, a .938 save percentage, 2 wins and a shutout. A shutout bonus of 3 points does plenty to a goalie line. Close behind, Jesper Wallstedt of MIN gave Charles 26.7 on 3 wins and an .944 mark over 84 saves. Joel Hofer of STL added 24.6 for Hammer with a shutout of his own. Devon Levi of EDM rounded out the list at 22.2 for Kegstands, stopping 49 of 50 for a .980 save percentage and a shutout. That is a lot of goaltending value on one roster: Saros and Levi between them supplied all four wins and both shutouts.

Power Rankings

by Dale Prychitko

Contenders

The top of the division is crowded, and the points column says it's tighter than the standings do. Four teams sit within about 33 points of each other on points for, and only one of them is still perfect.

#1 dMC — 7-0-0 (1.000) — W, 213.0-195.1

Seven wins and 1673.4 points for is a clean résumé, and 1440.2 against, tied for the second-lowest in the division, says the schedule has done them some favours. This week's 213.0 was only the seventh-highest score in the division, but it was enough to beat armdawg and stay unbeaten.

#2 Beef — 6-1-0 (.857) — W, 232.8-220.9

Beef leads the division in points for at 1675.4, a shade ahead of the unbeaten team above it, and it earned this week's win by outscoring Kegstands despite a 220.9 on the other side. The 1449.9 against shows a respectable schedule, so this record is earned.

#3 armdawg — 5-2-0 (.714) — L, 195.1-213.0

armdawg ran into the unbeaten team and posted one of the weaker scores of the week, 195.1. At 1644.9 points for, one soft week doesn't change the read on a legitimate contender.

#4 MazerReed — 5-2-0 (.714) — W, 248.9-208.5

The week's top score came from MazerReed, a 248.9 that put Sheeting Carlsson away without much argument. Another 1642.3 points for, a low 1420.9 against, and the easiest schedule of the group is worth keeping in mind, but a team that scores like this doesn't need much help.

Solid

Three teams that have been better than their records suggest, or at least comfortably above the middle of the pack.

#5 Hammer — 5-2-0 (.714) — W, 223.6-204.3

Hammer has five wins on 1525.0 points for, noticeably fewer than the contenders above, so the record is running ahead of the scoring. A 223.6 against Corey's Crazy Team was a solid week, but the points column asks for a little more.

#6 zzz — 4-3-0 (.571) — W, 236.1-194.2

zzz has scored 1546.5 points but has faced 1582.5 against, one of the harder schedules in the division, so a 4-3-0 record undersells the team. The 236.1 this week over Charles was the third-highest score on the board.

#7 Slew Foot Slim — 3-4-0 (.429) — W, 247.2-207.1

A 247.2 was the second-highest score of the week, and it came against HabsNation. Slew Foot Slim has 1549.6 points for to go with a 3-4-0 record, so the underlying numbers point to a team that has been a little unlucky.

Mediocre

Four teams tied at 3-4-0, separated mostly by how many points they've actually scored.

#8 Sheeting Carlsson — 3-4-0 (.429) — L, 208.5-248.9

Sheeting Carlsson scored 1545.1 points on the season, and 1440.2 against is a lighter schedule, so the losses are on the team. This week's 208.5 ran into the week's top score from MazerReed.

#9 Charles — 3-4-0 (.429) — L, 194.2-236.1

Charles has 1520.9 points for and 1526.1 against, an even split that explains a middling record. This week's 194.2 was the twelfth-highest score of fourteen, and zzz made it count.

#10 Kegstands — 3-4-0 (.429) — L, 220.9-232.8

A 220.9 was the sixth-highest score of the week and still lost to Beef. Kegstands has 1565.3 points against, the third-hardest schedule in the division by that total, and a 3-4-0 record owes something to it.

#11 Corey's Crazy Team — 3-4-0 (.429) — L, 204.3-223.6

Corey's Crazy Team has 1448.8 points for, the lowest of this group, and a 204.3 against Hammer didn't help. The 1544.2 against suggests some bad luck, but the scoring isn't strong enough to lean on that.

Rebuilding

Two wins between the bottom three all season, and the newest came in a week when nobody could score.

#12 I Walk for Sea Shanties — 1-6-0 (.143) — W, 172.1-159.9

I Walk for Sea Shanties picked up a win with a 172.1, the second-lowest score of the week, which is as ugly as a win gets. The 1500.2 against is a middle-of-the-pack schedule, and this week's opponent was the only team that scored less.

#13 HabsNation — 1-6-0 (.143) — L, 207.1-247.2

HabsNation scored 207.1 this week, which would have won plenty of matchups but ran into Slew Foot Slim's big week. A 1268.4 points for is the lowest in the division, and a 1552.3 against doesn't help.

#14 Walter White Walker — 0-7-0 (.000) — L, 159.9-172.1

Walter White Walker posted the lowest score of the week, 159.9, and lost to I Walk for Sea Shanties by 12.3 points. At 0-7-0 with 1615.4 points against, the hardest schedule in the division, the season has handed this team no breaks, though 1278.7 points for says it hasn't earned many either.

Movers and Shakers

by Dale Prychitko

Beef was the week's cleanest climb, moving up one spot to second at 6-1-0 and sitting a game clear of armdawg, who slipped a place to third at 5-2-0. Neither team looks like it is going anywhere soon, and the pair already have some daylight on the pack. Behind them the middle of the division is a logjam. zzz moved up two spots to sixth at 4-3-0, the only team in that stretch with a winning record to its name.

The biggest jump belongs to Slew Foot Slim, up four places to seventh at 3-4-0, which is a long way to travel for a team still under .500. Four other teams sit on that same 3-4-0 mark, and the shuffle shows how thin the margins are. Sheeting Carlsson and Charles each dropped two spots, to eighth and ninth, while Kegstands (tenth) and Corey's Crazy Team (eleventh) each lost a place. At the bottom, I Walk for Sea Shanties edged up one to twelfth and HabsNation slid to thirteenth, and both are stuck at 1-6-0. One good week would change a lot of those rankings.

Best Pickup of the Week

by Dale Prychitko

Slew Foot Slim takes the top two spots on the pickup board, and the crown goes to Simon Holmstrom. The NYI winger played three games in the active lineup and finished with 4 goals, 2 assists and 7 shots on goal. That is 28.8 fantasy points off the wire. Holmstrom has spent his career as a fringe name with real offensive tools, and this week the puck finally went in for him.

Credit Slew Foot Slim for more than one good grab. Victor Eklund, another NYI winger, came off the same wire and played the same three games. He put up 2 goals, 1 assist, 7 hits and 4 blocks for 18.3 fantasy points. Two productive pickups from one team is a week of waiver work most managers in this division can only envy.

Third place goes to MazerReed for Anthony Cirelli of TB. He played one game and picked up 3 assists for 10.0 fantasy points. That is a tidy return on a $7 bid for a centre who has long been a quiet, steady contributor.

Hall of Shame

by Dale Prychitko

Two of the three names in this week's Hall of Shame belong to dMC, and a columnist can only report what the waiver log shows. Shane Wright of NSH played two games in the active lineup and finished with no goals, no assists and 4.0 fantasy points. He fired four shots, added two hits and three blocks, and the puck stayed out of the net all week. For a former top prospect, that is a lot of volume with nothing to show for it.

Brayden McNabb of VGK was the other dMC add. The defenceman played two games and put up 4.3 fantasy points on 2 shots, 5 hits and 4 blocks. McNabb is a stay-at-home defender who has made a career out of hitting and blocking, and he did exactly that. The trouble is that a points league pays for those only in small change, so the bill came to 4.3 fantasy points.

Sheeting Carlsson rounds out the list with Jake DeBrusk of VAN. Two games, 5 shots, 5 hits, a block and no points, for 4.3 fantasy points. DeBrusk was dropped by zzz and scooped up by Sheeting Carlsson, so the wire did the same job twice over. Three pickups, one goal apiece wanted and none delivered. Every one of them was busy, and none of them was productive.

Goalie Stream of the Week

by Dale Prychitko

Tristan Jarry of EDM takes the goalie stream crown for armdawg. He turned aside 34 of 36 shots, which works out to a .944 save percentage, and picked up the win. That adds up to 10.2 fantasy points, and the saves alone did most of the heavy lifting. A goalie facing that much rubber who stops nearly all of it is the best kind of rental.

Walter White Walker found nearly as much with Casey DeSmith of DAL. He stopped 25 of 26 shots, a .962 save percentage, and earned the win for 9.0 fantasy points. The line was cleaner than Jarry's, with only one goal against, but fewer shots meant fewer save points. Facing 26 shots will do that.

armdawg gets the third spot as well, with Ville Husso of ANA. Husso stopped 20 of 21 for a .952 save percentage and a win, good for 7.5 fantasy points. Two of the three best streams this week came from the same manager, which says something about who was paying attention to the schedule. None of the three had a shutout, but all three won, and that is where the real money was.

Front Office Failures

by Dale Prychitko

Kegstands left Gabriel Vilardi on the bench on a day he had stats. He finished with 1 goal, 2 assists and 4 shots, worth 13.3 fantasy points that never reached the scoreboard. That is a big number to leave in a drawer. In a division where one matchup can come down to a handful of points, this one stings.

Walter White Walker benched Connor Hellebuyck on a day he had stats, and the cost was 9.9 fantasy points. Hellebuyck stopped 43 of 47 shots and earned the win. That is the kind of workload that turns into a big line, and it sat on the bench. A goalie who faces 47 shots is going to pay out, and the manager didn't collect.

Slew Foot Slim also left Tyler Toffoli on the bench for a day with 9.3 fantasy points on the line. The winger had a goal, an assist and 3 shots. Given how well the waiver wire treated this team, that is a small blemish on a good week.

The hot potato list is a busy one. dMC dropped Pierre-Luc Dubois, who went to Hammer and produced 6.0 fantasy points with a goal. zzz dropped Jake DeBrusk, who went to Sheeting Carlsson and managed 4.3. MazerReed dropped Luke Evangelista, who went to zzz and gave 4.3 fantasy points. Beef dropped Matt Coronato, who landed with MazerReed. None of those stung much on their own, but that is a lot of churn for very little production.

Transaction Desk

by Dale Prychitko

The lone trade this week sent Brady Skjei of NSH to dMC and Sean Walker of CAR to Sheeting Carlsson. It is a swap of defencemen, one for one, and neither side gave up depth to get it. Both are the kind of blueliner who piles up shots, blocks and the odd point. I lean dMC here: Skjei carries a bigger offensive role and the shot volume that goes with it. Walker is a fine player, but Skjei has the edge in what the points scoring pays for.

On the FAAB side, the week was quiet. Three bids totalled $18. MazerReed led the way with a $7 bid on Anthony Cirelli of TB, dropping Luke Evangelista, and got 10.0 fantasy points from three assists in one game. That is money well spent. MazerReed also bid $5 on Matt Coronato of CGY, dropping Philipp Grubauer, and sits at $72 remaining.

zzz bid $6 on Mason McTavish of STL, dropping Jake DeBrusk, and got 4.3 fantasy points from 5 shots, a hit and 3 blocks. That is a thin return, and it matters because zzz has $37 left, the lowest budget in the division. dMC is next at $42, with Corey's Crazy Team at $50. A manager running low this early in the season has less room to chase a breakout later.

At the other end, Beef, I Walk for Sea Shanties and HabsNation still hold the full $100, and Walter White Walker has $99. Those managers have spent almost nothing, which means they have a lot of ammunition if someone good shakes loose.

Expected Points

by Priya Raman

Kegstands put up the sixth-highest score of the week and lost anyway. Kegstands posted 220.9 against Beef's 232.8, a total above the 211.7 division average and higher than what dMC, Sheeting Carlsson, HabsNation and Corey's Crazy Team scored. The goaltending was elite: 188 saves, the most in the division, plus two shutouts and four wins. The skaters let it down, with six goals and 25 points on the week against 13 goals and 38 points for Beef. A goalie can carry a roster only so far when the forwards barely register.

The opposite case is at the bottom of the board. I Walk for Sea Shanties won with 172.1, the second-lowest score of the week, because Walter White Walker managed only 159.9. The goalie lines are the story. I Walk for Sea Shanties logged 12 saves and one goal against all week. Walter White Walker had 142 saves, four wins and a shutout, but its skaters finished with the division's lowest totals in goals (4), shots (68) and points (20). Four wins and a shutout from the crease still lost to a team whose goaltending barely showed up. That is a week of variance in the matchup column and not much else.

dMC led the division in shots (119) and blocked shots (39) and beat armdawg, 213.0-195.1. armdawg had the most wins of anyone with four and 132 saves, but its 24 hits were the fewest in the division and its skaters couldn't keep pace on volume. Shot and block totals like those come from deployment and role, which tends to repeat, while a four-win goalie week usually doesn't.

Elsewhere the scoreboard behaved. MazerReed topped the division at 248.9 on 40 points and 16 goals, with Slew Foot Slim right behind at 247.2 and the week's high of 18 goals. Corey's Crazy Team is worth watching: 37 points and 13 goals in a 204.3 loss to Hammer, with zero goalie wins and 55 saves all week. The skaters did their job. The crease didn't produce a single win.
