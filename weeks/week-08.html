Matchup Recaps

by Dale Prychitko

Jack and Co. spent the first half of the week looking up at HabsNation. After Wednesday, HabsNation led 91.5-65.3, a 26.2-point cushion. Thursday flipped it, with Jack and Co. moving ahead 142.3-129.8, and they never gave the lead back. They finished 242.7-177.7. Robert Thomas led the roster with 38.0 fantasy points and Brock Boeser added 33.5, and Jack and Co. also had 32 assists on the week. HabsNation got 30.3 points from Vasily Podkolzin, but the second half of the week was lopsided.

armdawg and zzz traded the lead three times, and armdawg won 225.6-191.0. zzz held a half-point edge after Monday, armdawg took the lead Tuesday, and zzz reclaimed it Thursday, 120.1-108.9. armdawg pulled ahead again Saturday, 179.0-164.4, and added to it on Sunday. Roman Kantserov (30.8), Jimmy Snuggerud (29.3) and Cutter Gauthier (28.3) carried the depth, and armdawg chipped in 26 assists. Lane Hutson's 29.3 points were the best on the zzz roster.

The tightest finish was Sheeting Carlsson 254.0-242.5 over Slew Foot Slim, an 11.5-point margin. Matt Boldy (35.5) and Frank Nazar (32.3) did the damage for Sheeting Carlsson. Slew Foot Slim put up 139 saves and four wins, and Spencer Knight chipped in 20.4 points, but the skaters were thin on goals with 15 on the week. Philip Broberg's 28.0 from the blue line was the best of them. Goaltending kept Slew Foot Slim close and the scoring ran out.

Walter White Walker is the strangest line of the week. The goalies stopped 271 shots, won five games and recorded a shutout, and Jake Oettinger alone gave them 28.2 points. The skaters managed 5 goals and 6 assists, and the team lost 195.9-130.3 to Corey's Crazy Team, which got 30 assists (10 of them from the blue line) and 50 hits. Mikko Rantanen's 24.3 points led Corey's Crazy Team. MazerReed beat Kegstands 269.5-220.6, the biggest total of the week. Tage Thompson had 38.0 fantasy points and Leon Draisaitl 32.0, and the skaters combined for 48 points (goals plus assists). Matthew Schaefer's 30.3 kept Kegstands in range.

Beef beat I Walk for Sea Shanties 242.9-188.7 and led the week's winners with 58 hits and 3 shorthanded points. Alex Tuch (26.0) and Vincent Trocheck (25.5) set the pace. John Carlson's 22.0 was the best of I Walk for Sea Shanties, whose team managed only 33 saves. dMC beat Hammer 194.1-156.4 without much goaltending, with 26 saves and one win. Joel Eriksson Ek (28.8) and Auston Matthews (25.3) did most of the scoring, and 49 hits and 43 blocks filled in the rest. Hammer got 113 saves and four wins but only 22 points from its skaters. Sam Rinzel's 34.5 was the one big skater line.

Players of the Week

by Dale Prychitko

Robert Thomas of Jack and Co. takes Skater of the Week with 38.0 fantasy points, and the line is built on a playmaker's work: 10 points on 2 goals and 8 assists, plus 8 shots on goal. The St. Louis centre has always been a pass-first player, and a week where the helpers outnumber the goals four to one suits him. Tage Thompson of MazerReed finished with the same 38.0 and has a fair complaint about the tiebreak. The Buffalo centre fired 16 shots and scored 5 goals, and the shot volume alone was worth plenty on top of the goals. Thompson has had that kind of shooting touch when he's healthy and rolling, and this week he was both.

Matt Boldy of Sheeting Carlsson was next at 35.5 points, with 4 goals and 4 assists for the Minnesota winger. Sam Rinzel of Hammer rounds out the skater podium at 34.5, and his line is the one that deserves a second look. The young Chicago defenceman had 1 goal and 7 assists, 11 shots, 4 hits and 5 blocks. A defenceman who adds shots, hits and blocks to a steady stream of assists is worth more than his position label suggests.

Jake Oettinger of Walter White Walker is Goalie of the Week with 28.2 fantasy points. He won 3 games, stopped 84 of 90 shots for a .933 save percentage, and picked up a shutout along the way. The Dallas netminder made a lot of saves and gave up very few goals, and that combination is how a goalie leaves the rest of the pack behind. Spencer Knight of Slew Foot Slim was second at 20.4 points on the strength of volume, with 93 saves on 102 shots for 2 wins. Jesper Wallstedt of Jack and Co. posted 15.9 points and Andrei Vasilevskiy of HabsNation 15.6 points. Vasilevskiy's .940 was the best save percentage among the runners-up, and he gave up only 3 goals. Jack and Co. had the week's top skater and a goalie on the board, so Charles got value from both ends of the lineup.

Movers and Shakers

by Dale Prychitko

MazerReed is the story at the top of the table. A move up one spot puts them third at 6-2-0, and armdawg slides down one to fourth with the same record. The two sit level on wins, so the order between them is a thin one, and neither has much room to coast. Both are clear of the logjam below them.

The middle of the division is a four-way scramble at 4-4-0, and it shuffled hard. Sheeting Carlsson and Jack and Co. each climbed two spots, to sixth and seventh. Best Pickup of the Week

by Dale Prychitko

Hammer takes the top pickup this week with Sam Rinzel, the young CHI defenceman who piled up 34.5 fantasy points in four games, all in the active lineup. He scored 1 goal, added 7 assists for 8 points, and put 11 shots on net. He also chipped in 4 hits and 5 blocks, which is the kind of well-rounded line a defenceman needs to pay off in this scoring system. Rinzel has long been billed as an offensive talent, and this week he looked like it. A tip of the cap to MPR for grabbing him before anyone else noticed.

Sheeting Carlsson finished a close second with Frank Nazar, another CHI skater. The centre scored 4 goals and 2 assists on 13 shots for 32.3 fantasy points over four games. Nazar's goal total is the bigger number, but Rinzel's assists and blocks gave him the edge. Dev has little to be ashamed of here.

Beef rounds out the podium with Logan Mailloux of STL. The defenceman put up 21.5 fantasy points in three games on 1 goal, 3 assists, 8 shots and 10 hits. The hits are the unglamorous part, and they add up. Liam found a defenceman who contributes in a lot of places.

HabsNation picked up Dawson Mercer of WPG, and Mercer gave back 3.0 fantasy points in three games. He had 5 shots and 2 hits and no goals or assists, so the whole line fits in a text message. Goalie Stream of the Week

by Dale Prychitko

Anthony Stolarz of TOR is the best goalie stream of the week, and there's a catch: it's a low bar. He played two games for armdawg and finished with 6.0 fantasy points, which tops the list. He stopped 55 of 62 shots for a .887 save percentage, allowed 7 goals and didn't pick up a win or a shutout. The saves carried the whole line, since the volume at 0.3 per stop outweighed the goals against.

Walter White Walker used Daniil Tarasov of DET and got 3.6 fantasy points from it. He saved 27 of 30 shots, a .900 mark, with 3 goals against and no win. It's a decent night's work, nothing more. Tony got his points out of a single game.

Hammer rounds out the group with Michael DiPietro of BOS, who turned in 2.7 fantasy points on 19 saves from 21 shots, with 2 goals against. His .905 save percentage was the best of the three, but without a win it only went so far. MPR got a respectable performance out of a stream that never got him a win.

Front Office Failures

by Dale Prychitko

Start with Kegstands, who left Elias Lindholm on the bench on a day he scored 4 goals and added an assist. That's 24.5 fantasy points sitting on the pine, the biggest single hit on this week's list. Lindholm's line also included 6 shots and a block, and every one of those points went to waste. Somebody in that lineup got the nod instead, and the data says it wasn't a good call.

I Walk for Sea Shanties managed to bench three productive players on days they produced. Sam Bennett had 3 goals, an assist and a shorthanded point for 21.0 fantasy points, David Pastrnak had 13.0, and Ivar Stenberg had 9.8. That's a lot of points left on the bench by Matthew, and the lineup ought to have been checked more carefully. When three players can all produce on a bench day, the lineup deserves a second look.

Kegstands shows up again with Trevor Connelly, who had 9.8 fantasy points on a day he sat, on 2 assists and 6 shots. Beef left Maxim Shabanov on the bench for three days in which he scored a goal, fired 5 shots and blocked 3, worth 8.8 fantasy points. HabsNation rounds out the list with Jake Neighbours, who had 2 assists and 4 shots for 8.8 fantasy points while on the bench.
